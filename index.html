Penne ragù in 30 minutes

One pot, a can of tomatoes and half an hour. This is the pasta I make when I can’t be bothered to cook but still want a proper dinner.

Method

6 steps · about 30 minutes

  1. 01
    Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil for the pasta.
  2. 02
    Finely chop the onion and crush the garlic. Sweat the onion in olive oil until soft, add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds more.
    4 min
  3. 03
    Add the meat and brown it over high heat, breaking it up with a spoon.
    6 min
  4. 04
    Stir in the tomato paste and cook for a minute. Add the tomatoes, oregano and chili. Simmer on low heat, then season with salt and pepper.
    10 min
  5. 05
    Meanwhile, cook the penne al dente according to the package. Save half a cup of the pasta water.
  6. 06
    Add the pasta to the sauce with a splash of the pasta water and toss until the sauce coats every piece. Serve topped with grated parmesan.

Tap a step to mark it as done.

A little tip

Don’t pour all the pasta water down the drain. The starch in it makes the sauce creamy and helps it cling to the penne instead of pooling at the bottom of the plate.
