Spaghetti al pomodoro in 20 minutes

Sweet cherry tomatoes, garlic and a fistful of basil. A few ingredients, twenty minutes, and the best argument for keeping things simple.

Method

6 steps · about 20 minutes

  1. 01
    Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil for the pasta.
  2. 02
    Halve the tomatoes and thinly slice the garlic. Gently warm the olive oil with the garlic and chili until the garlic just turns golden.
    2 min
  3. 03
    Add the tomatoes with a pinch of salt and cook over medium heat, pressing them with a spoon, until they collapse into a sauce.
    10 min
  4. 04
    Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti until just shy of al dente. Save a cup of the pasta water.
  5. 05
    Move the spaghetti straight into the sauce with a splash of the pasta water and toss until glossy.
    2 min
  6. 06
    Off the heat, stir in torn basil and grated parmesan. Serve with more basil and parmesan on top.

Tap a step to mark it as done.

A little tip

Finish the pasta in the sauce, not on the plate. A minute of tossing with starchy pasta water turns tomatoes and oil into a silky sauce that clings to every strand.
